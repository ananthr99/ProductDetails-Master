RTXX

Industrial 5G/4G Dual Modem Router

The RTXX is a compact industrial router featuring MediaTek MT7981 with Wi-Fi 6, dual M.2 modem slots supporting 5G/4G/3G, and OpenWRT Linux. Designed for demanding IoT, industrial automation, and edge networking deployments, it supports dual SIM with 4 LAN and 1 WAN Gigabit Ethernet ports in a fanless DIN-rail sheet-metal enclosure with wide 9–30 VDC input.
