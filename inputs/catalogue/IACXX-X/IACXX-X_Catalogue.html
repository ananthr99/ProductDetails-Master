Industrial 4G Gateway

A slim DIN-rail industrial gateway on MT7628 with 4G single-SIM, optional Wi-Fi 4, RS485, DI/O, and 5 Fast Ethernet ports running OpenWRT Linux. Suited for OEM embedded connectivity, factory floor network integration, and infrastructure equipment deployments.
