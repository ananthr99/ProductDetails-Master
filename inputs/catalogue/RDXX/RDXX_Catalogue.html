Industrial 4G Router

A rugged industrial cellular router on MediaTek MT7628 with 4G dual-SIM failover, optional Wi-Fi 4, RS485 serial interface, and OpenWRT Linux. Designed for IoT, smart infrastructure, transportation, and enterprise branch networking requiring secure, always-on connectivity.
