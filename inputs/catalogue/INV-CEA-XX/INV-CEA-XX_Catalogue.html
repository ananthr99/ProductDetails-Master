INV-CEA-XX

Industrial 5G/4G Dual Modem Edge Computer

The INV-CEA-XX is a fanless industrial gateway designed for high-performance edge computing and robust connectivity, featuring an Intel X7425E processor and extensive cellular/Wi-Fi expansion capabilities. It differentiates itself through quad-Gigabit Ethernet ports, dual isolated serial interfaces (RS232/RS485), and flexible mounting options, making it highly adaptable for harsh environments. Primarily targeted at industrial automation, telecommunications, and smart city infrastructure where reliable long-term operation is critical.
