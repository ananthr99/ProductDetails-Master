Industrial 5G/4G Dual Modem Router

A ruggedised industrial router featuring dual 5G/4G modems, Wi-Fi 6, and SFP uplinks. Built on MediaTek MT7981 with OpenWRT, it supports dual-SIM failover, DIN-rail mounting, and a wide 9–30V DC power range — ideal for demanding telecom, industrial, and remote site deployments.
