ROXX

Industrial 5G/4G Dual Modem Router

A ruggedised industrial router combining dual 5G/4G modems with Wi-Fi 6 (EasyMesh), 2× WAN + 4× LAN GbE ports, and 1× SFP uplink. Built on MediaTek MT7981 with OpenWRT 21/23, supporting dual-SIM failover, DIN-rail mounting, and a wide 9–30V DC input — engineered for demanding telecom, industrial, and enterprise edge deployments.
