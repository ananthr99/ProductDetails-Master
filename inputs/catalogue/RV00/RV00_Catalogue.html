RV00

Industrial - Router

The RV00 is a compact industrial router built on MediaTek MT7621 featuring Wi-Fi 4, five Gigabit Ethernet ports, and OpenWRT Linux with VPN, VLAN, and NMS remote management. Ideal for industrial IoT and M2M deployments without cellular connectivity.
