Industrial 5G/4G Edge Computer

Typical Applications / Use Cases

Remote Industrial IoT GatewayThe 4G/5G cellular connectivity paired with 4 Gigabit Ethernet ports lets it aggregate data from multiple field sensors and PLCs, then backhaul it over cellular where wired internet isn't available. Its -20°C to 75°C operating range and fanless design suit outdoor cabinets or remote plant sites.
Retail & Branch Edge ComputingWi-Fi 6 support and flexible mounting (wall, desktop, or 19" rack) make it easy to deploy as a local compute node in stores or branch offices for POS systems, digital signage, or local analytics. The Intel x7425E processor with up to 64GB RAM handles on-site processing without relying on cloud latency.
Vehicle & Transportation TelematicsDual Nano SIM slots and 5G readiness enable reliable connectivity for fleet vehicles or mobile units even while roaming across networks. The compact, rugged aluminum enclosure and 12VDC input make it well-suited for invehicle mounting and automotive power systems.
