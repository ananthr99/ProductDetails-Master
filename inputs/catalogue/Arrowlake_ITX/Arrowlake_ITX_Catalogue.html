Industrial - Edge Computer

The Invendis ARL Mini ITX is a high-performance industrial computing platform powered by Intel Core Ultra Processors (Series 2) with integrated NPU AI acceleration, Intel Arc 140T graphics, and up to 128 GB DDR5 RAM. Housed in an aluminium heatsink enclosure with fanless or fan options, it offers dual 2.5 GbE LAN ports, triple display outputs (2× HDMI, 1× DP), and extensive M.2 expansion for Wi-Fi 6, 4G/5G cellular, and NVMe storage.

Typical Applications / Use Cases

AI-Powered Edge Vision & Video AnalyticsIntegrated NPU acceleration and optional Intel Arc 140T GPU allow real-time inference for surveillance analytics, defect detection, or people-counting directly on-site, without sending video to the cloud. Dual 2.5GbE ports support multiple high-bandwidth camera feeds simultaneously.
Industrial IoT Gateway & Automation ControllerTriple display outputs (2x HDMI, 1x DP) let operators monitor SCADA dashboards, camera walls, and diagnostics across multiple screens from one compact unit. Up to 128GB DDR5 RAM and NVMe storage handle demanding multitasking and data logging workloads.
High-Performance Interactive Kiosks & Digital Signage5G/4G cellular connectivity combined with a fanless or fan-optional aluminum enclosure suits deployment in vehicles, kiosks, or remote sites needing high compute power without a data center.
